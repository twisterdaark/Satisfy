# The class audit (2026-10-26)

The owner: "Go through every single class in-game, confirming they have their skills from the wiki and the novel. Especially
the Legendary Ones." Four research passes over the novel (56 classes, 318 skills). His choices the same night:

- **Pagma's Sword Dance**: add its passive effect beside the dances (the dances stay always available).
- **Numbers**: the novel's damage and effects, our cooldowns.
- **Sword Energy**: add it.

Sources: wuxiaworld's chapter pages show each chapter's opening only (ch 2-4 and 15-16 whole); the fandom wiki, archive.org,
ko namu.wiki and novel mirrors refuse us. So a skill whose window lies past an opening is "not found", not "not canon"; no
number is taken from memory. [F chN] a fetched chapter; [S] the search index only.
