the Legendary Ones." Four research passes over the novel (56 classes, 318 skills). His choices the same night:

- **Pagma's Sword Dance**: add its passive effect beside the dances (the dances stay always available).

Sources: wuxiaworld's chapter pages show each chapter's opening only (ch 2-4 and 15-16 whole); the fandom wiki, archive.org,
ko namu.wiki and novel mirrors refuse us. So a skill whose window lies past an opening is "not found", not "not canon"; no
number is taken from memory. [F chN] a fetched chapter; [S] the search index only.

## Open (needs the chapters' text, or the owner's call)

- Hell Leap is a fast combat move in the novel (ch 754, 1536, 1683); ours is a 15 s trip to the Fourth Ridge.
- Hell Regulation halves a great demon's magic power (ch 573) and is a field for allies (ch 1490).
- The Demon Slayer's Demonic Essence (ch 574) and buff removal (ch 947).
- Condemnation Sword: "attack power equivalent to 100% of the target's defense" (ch 772): our monsters have no defence stat.
- Sanctuary Declaration takes an hour to build in the novel (ch 1461, 1463).
- Pagma's Successor wears any gear in the novel but "there is a penalty depending on the rating" (ch 3, 16): ours has no penalty.
- Holy Weapon in the novel is +500 divine damage a hit (ch 495): ours stays a share (+20%, +50% on corrupt beings), since a flat 500 means little at level 300.
- Braham's Successor is the game's own class (the owner's, 2026-10-22); the novel's is Grid's Assimilation into Legendary Great Magician (ch 520). Its spells carry "(Enhanced)" in the novel.
- Passing the Sky, Thunderbolt and Twin Fang Strike: their windows weren't reached.
- Classes the game lacks: Debirion's Envoy, Sky Rider (ch 1044), Death God (ch 831), Wind Magician (ch 1043).
