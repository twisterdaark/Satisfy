# Satisfy: Pagma's Descendant (Prototype)

A light, fan-made PC game prototype inspired by the Overgeared manhwa/anime and the world of Satisfy.
Runs in the browser, Arabic and English.

**Play:** https://twisterdaark.github.io/Overgeared/

## Controls
WASD / arrows move · J or left click attack · Space dodge · E talk/mine/anvil · I inventory · 1 eat · Esc menu

Fan project, not affiliated with the Overgeared rights holders.
